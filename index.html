About

I'm a drilling engineer with nine years at TotalEnergies and Perenco, working on offshore and onshore wells in Gabon, Uganda, Suriname, Tanzania and France. Most recently, as Senior Drilling Engineer at Perenco, I owned execution across 14 wells with a combined budget of US$190 million and cut non-productive time by more than 15%.

I like pairing hands-on rig work with better tools, from drilling-engineering software to a machine-learning model that predicts operational issues. I'm now doing an MBA at INSEAD (class of July 2027) to take that operational experience into broader leadership roles.
